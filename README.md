# 🧭 Compass Portfolio — Jhun Ray Omiping

> *“A ship is safe in harbor, but that’s not what ships are for.”*

This is the source code for my **interactive, gamified portfolio** built with Three.js, GSAP, and a touch of RPG‑style progression. The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) and reflects my journey as a software engineer, support analyst, and lifelong learner.

## 🌐 Live Website

Explore the full experience at **[JhunRay.com](https://jhunray.github.io/Portfolio/index.html)** (or the deployed URL of your choice).

## ✨ Features

- **3D Compass Rose** – Animated, rotatable compass built with Three.js that reacts to mouse movement and scroll.
- **Gamification System** – Collect floating orange stars to increase your **Score** and **Level**. Level‑up notifications add a playful touch.
- **Scroll‑Triggered Animations** – Sections fade in as you scroll, and each new section awards bonus points.
- **Floating Collectibles** – Stars respawn dynamically; tap or click them to earn points.
- **Fully Responsive** – Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile (with touch‑friendly collectibles).
- **Navigation Dots** – Quick anchor links to jump between sections.
- **Project Showcase** – Highlights my **Leave Management System** (capstone) and **Tipidify Budget Tracker** (published on Google Play).

## 🎮 Gamification Mechanics

- **Collectibles** – Orange circles appear randomly on screen. Each gives **+10 points**.
- **Score → Level** – Every 100 points increases your level by 1.
- **First Visit Bonus** – +20 points when the page loads.
- **Section Discovery** – Visiting a new section for the first time gives **+25 points**.
- **Level‑Up Effect** – A toast message and panel pulse animation celebrate your progression.
